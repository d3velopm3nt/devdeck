18:00 What did I learn
The machine is counted, not listed
Three bots waking at eight every weekday is three identical lines a day and sixty a month. It buried the two things on the month that were about you. One green line says the same and gives the room back.
A deadline is first, whatever hour it claims
On a month the question is what lands this week, never at what minute. Overdue is red, coming is amber, and both sit above everything else in the cell.
A day is a door
The number, and “+n more”, open that day. Zooming in on what you just pointed at is the one navigation a month owes you — and it is where the times live.
