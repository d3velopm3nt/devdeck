What the icon does
Quiet by default. It only lights up for something you can act on — a bot that lights up for its own routine work is a bot you learn to ignore.

The rule
One step per reason, and the reason is always something you could do next.
asleep, or working — no attention
work waiting, an approval, a deadline today
blocked, overdue, or a session that failed
A lit bot always has a matching card under Needs you, so the icon is a pointer and never the only place a thing is said. Clearing it there puts the light out.
