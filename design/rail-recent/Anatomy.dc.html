The three pieces
What changes, and why each one.

Labels become pills

Now Product Pill Product Topic Archive

Fully rounded, and tinted rather than grey. The tint is derived from the label text, so the same word is the same colour everywhere without anyone choosing one — and a label you invent gets a colour for free.

Solutions in the rail become Recent

That list has been empty since folders became real: it filters for a solution kind the scanner stopped producing. Rather than resurrect a kind we deliberately removed, the slot holds the last three folders you opened.

It keeps what you liked — a short jump list next to Home — without needing a category to maintain. Clicking one scopes the Explorer to it, exactly as picking a solution did.

Ordered by when you last opened one, so it reorders itself and never needs curating.

The signals were built already — running, behind, working, waiting — but only rendered when one was non-zero. A quiet workspace looked untouched, which is why it read as never built.

Now the second line is always present, falling back to the project count. And the icon becomes the workspace’s own initials in its own colour, so the tabs are told apart by shape rather than by reading them.
