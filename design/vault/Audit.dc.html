What this table cannot tell you
That a process, once handed a value, did the thing it said it would with it. Vault controls the door; it does not follow anyone around the room.
Which is why Use is granted per item and per agent rather than in bulk, why grants name a command, and why the four rows above that went nowhere are worth as much as the ones that did.
