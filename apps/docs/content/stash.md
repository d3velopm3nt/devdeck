# Stash and knowledge

Stash captures clipboard material and supports saved clips and screenshots. The capture classifier is intended to detect secret-shaped content, but check sensitive material before saving or sharing it.

## Knowledge

Knowledge makes space context searchable and available to agents. Store useful source material near its project and provide explicit links in task instructions.

## Example

Capture a useful reference, associate it with the project, and link it from the feature brief so a worker knows why it matters.
