A safe order
1Redraw the day
Heights, lanes, layers, the now line, the deadline rail. No schema change, no new command — today's data, drawn honestly. Reversible in an afternoon.
2Two small columns
Reminder lead time and the goal link, plus the focus-hours sum. Each is one migration and one field in the sheet.
3Habits and free slots
The real new feature, and the one that makes the day view something you use rather than read. Build it whole or not at all — half a habit tracker is a to-do list that nags.
4Write runs down
Persist agent sessions so the past has agent time in it. Until then the agents lane is honest only about today, and should say so rather than show an empty yesterday.
