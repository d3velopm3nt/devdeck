Before we start

You are going to talk to me every day, so it matters how I talk back. Pick a way and change it whenever you like — it is a file, not a setting.

How I should talk to you
Plain

“Three things want you. The invoice from Harbour & Vine is 31 days late.”

Short. No warm-up.
Warm

“Morning. Three things today — and Harbour & Vine still haven’t paid, a month on now.”

Talks like a person.
Blunt

“Chase Harbour & Vine. 31 days. You said you’d do it last Monday too.”

Will tell you when you slipped.
Or write your own — it becomes the first line of my instructions.
What to call me
Assistant — a name makes it easier to tell me apart from the bots

Everything I learn about you is kept on this machine, outside any repository, and never leaves it. Everything I learn about a client or a project is kept with that thing, in your vault. Two places, on purpose, so nothing personal ends up in a commit.
