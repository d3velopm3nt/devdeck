Why not one form with a kind dropdown
Because the fields are not the same fields. A focus session has no repeat, a habit has no start time, and a handover has an owner. A single form would show you eight fields to fill in three.
What the tabs cost us
Event and Routine ship as they are — both are schedules rows today. Focus needs a start time it does not have. Habit needs a table. Ask a bot needs the delegate path wired to a due date.
The one refusal
There is no “wake agent X at 9am”. It was left out on purpose and stays out: a naked agent on a timer has no goal, no place to write and nobody to ask. The bot tab is the supported shape of that wish.
