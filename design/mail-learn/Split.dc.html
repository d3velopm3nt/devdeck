What is never written
•   The messages. They are already in SQLite and stay a cache — delete it and a sync rebuilds it.
•   Summaries you did not accept. A proposal that is declined leaves a “no” behind, not a note.
•   Anything the secret-shape rule flagged, on either side.
•   A silent profile. Every fact here has a Keep button in front of it and a source under it.

The second fact on this board is the argument for reading bodies at all. No header, signature or sender ranking could ever tell you that a client agreed to thirty days and pays at forty-five. That sentence is somewhere in a thread and in a contract, and it is the sort of thing you actually wanted DevDeck to know.
